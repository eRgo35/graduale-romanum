database of Gregorian scores maintained by its community of transcribers and
proofreaders. Each file in `missa-viii/gabc/` is based on the entry below; the
original transcriber of every one is Andrew Hinkley.

## Reference editions

- *Vatican II Hymnal*, Corpus Christi Watershed, 2011, pp. 163–167: pitch,
  slurs, bars, text and layout of the modern-notation Mass VIII.
- [Gregorianum.org, *Missa VIII (Missa de Angelis)*](https://en.gregorianum.org/wiki/Missa_VIII_(Missa_de_Angelis)):
  gabc of the whole Mass (Credo III transcribed by Andrew Hinkley and Stefano
  Ferri), used to cross-check pitches.
- *The Liber Usualis*, Solesmes, 1961, pp. 68–70: Credo III verified note by
  note against the
  [scan published by the Church Music Association of America](https://archive.org/details/TheLiberUsualis1961).
- *Graduale Romanum*, Solesmes, 1961 and 1974: the Kyriale editions cited in
  the gabc headers.

## Texts
